# Afri Index
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Explore African artists through source-backed rankings, streaming observations and reported live performance. Touring remains independent of existing Current and All-Time scores.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Extend the existing generated HTML, vanilla JavaScript, Node API and Supabase architecture. Preserve authentication, playback, newsletter, biographies and ranking formulas. No new paid services or serverless functions. Published touring figures require verified source records and reuse permission. The owner confirmed that no licensed touring dataset is currently available.

Additional recurring budget is zero naira. No subscription, paid API, plan upgrade, paid scraper, continuous scraping or new scheduled job is permitted. Confirmed concerts remain visible even when finances are not publicly reported. Track African subregions and worldwide destinations separately from artist nationality and listener geography. Manually verified public facts and approved imports can expand the dataset without a commercial feed.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve Afri Index's existing premium dark identity, shared typography, lime accent, light theme and navigation conventions. Prefer compact data surfaces over decorative cards. Support 360px and 390px screens, keyboard operation and reduced motion.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing artist IDs, photographs, Spotify catalogue observations and source dates. No touring box-office data is approved for publication. Empty states must distinguish missing evidence from zero activity. Do not fabricate historic movement, listener geography, conversion rates or overall winners.

## Product Principles
- Report what the evidence covers.
- Keep platforms, periods and measurement units separate.
- Supersede revisions and reject overlapping event coverage.
- Keep incomplete reporting visible and attributable.
