How ranks 51 to 100 are chosen and scored

Candidates come from the Spotify weekly charts for Nigeria and South Africa (via Kworb) and from the All-Time 50. Each needs a Wikidata identity with an African country, linked by the exact Spotify artist ID or by a name that matches only one musician. Streams come from the artist’s Kworb ledger.

Score: 45% recent streams, 35% momentum, 10% editorial, 10% activity. New candidates have no momentum baseline yet, so momentum is neutral until they have a few weekly readings. Rows marked “Not yet reviewed” have no bio, editorial score or review date yet.
