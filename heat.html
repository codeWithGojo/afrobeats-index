How Heat 25 works

Momentum compares this week’s daily Spotify reading with the average of the artist’s readings over the 4 weeks before. +100% means streams doubled. The pool is the Current 50. Artists whose source has not updated in 14 days, or who have no baseline yet, sit out until they do. Heat never changes the Current 50. Full method.

History note: this edition’s baseline is 14 Sep to 5 Oct because weekly history paused until 22 Nov. It tightens to the prior 4 weeks as editions build up.
