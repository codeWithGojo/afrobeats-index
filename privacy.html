Last updated 24 November 2026

Who is responsible

Afri Index is operated by an individual in Nigeria, who is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: imegufavour30@gmail.com. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Browsing without an account

You can read the rankings, charts and artist pages without an account. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies and reports aggregated visits, not individual profiles. Some preferences, such as your colour theme, saved pages and an offline copy of the chart, are kept only in your browser's storage on your device.

If you create an account

Accounts are provided through Supabase. We store your email address, a username you choose and, only if you use these features: your board, saved Music Receipts (and whether each is public), comments and reports, correction suggestions with their source links, poll votes, chart predictions, artists you follow and push-notification subscriptions. A public receipt or board is visible to anyone with its link; private ones are visible only to you.

Music Receipt and Spotify

If you build a receipt manually or import a CSV, JSON or pasted history, that listening data stays in your browser and is not uploaded unless you save or share the receipt. If you connect Spotify, we request read access to your top artists and tracks. The access token is kept in memory in your browser for that session and is not stored on our servers.

Your African music stats

The stats page only works after you tick a consent box. You can connect Spotify (read access to your top items and recently played, scopes user-top-read and user-read-recently-played), give a public Last.fm username, or choose your Spotify streaming history file.

  • Matching happens in your browser. Only plays by African artists in our index are kept. Everything else is counted as unmatched and discarded.
  • If you are signed in, we store: the source you chose, your consent time, the date tracking started, each African play (time, artist, track title), a count of unmatched plays and, only if you choose to share it, a handle and your top 20 African artists.
  • Spotify tokens stay in your browser. If you tick “keep tracking”, the refresh token is kept in this browser’s storage so the page can fetch new plays when you return. It is never sent to our servers.
  • History files contain sensitive data such as IP addresses and devices. They are read only in your browser and never uploaded. Pro members can save the African-artist totals from them, never the file.
  • Sharing is off by default. A shared taste profile shows your handle and top 20 African artists to people who look up your handle, and lets you appear in other people’s soulmate matches.
  • Delete everything from this feature at any time with “Delete my stats data” on the stats page.

The Apple Music waitlist stores your email only if you tick the box, and we email you once when it is ready.

The Sunday brief

If you subscribe, your email address and your consent choices (the weekly brief, and optional sponsor offers, which are off by default) are sent to Buttondown, which delivers the newsletter. You must confirm by email, and every issue has an unsubscribe link. Account notifications are sent through Resend.

Sponsors

Sponsored placements are labelled. We count how often each placement is seen and clicked as daily totals per slot; no reader data is shared with sponsors. If you send a sponsorship inquiry, we keep your name, company, email and message to reply to you, and delete them on request.

Pro payments

Pro payments are processed by our payment provider (Flutterwave, or Paystack if enabled). Card and bank details are entered on the provider's checkout page and never reach our servers. We store an order reference, the amount, the currency, the time it was paid and your Pro expiry date. API keys you create are stored only as one-way hashes; we cannot see the full key after it is shown to you.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: database and sign-in (EU region).
  • Vercel: website hosting and cookieless analytics.
  • Buttondown: newsletter subscriptions and delivery.
  • Resend: account email delivery.
  • Flutterwave / Paystack: payment processing.
  • Spotify: only if you choose to connect your account.
  • Last.fm: only if you enter a Last.fm username; we request that public profile’s top items and recent plays.

We do not sell personal data, and rankings are not influenced by sponsors or by anyone's personal data.

Why we use your data

We use account and receipt data to provide the features you ask for; newsletter data on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time; and payment records to provide Pro access and meet accounting obligations.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you ask us to delete your account by emailing imegufavour30@gmail.com. Deleting an account removes its profile, receipts, comments, votes, predictions, follows, API keys and personal stats. Personal stats can also be deleted on their own from the stats page at any time. Payment records are kept as long as needed for accounting and tax purposes. Newsletter data is kept by Buttondown until you unsubscribe and ask for deletion.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to or restrict its use. Email imegufavour30@gmail.com from the address on your account. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children

Afri Index is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when our practices change. Significant changes affecting account holders will also be announced on the site.
