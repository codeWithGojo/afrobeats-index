About the service

Afri Index is operated by an individual based in Nigeria. It publishes rankings, charts and artist information about African music, with tools such as Music Receipt, comparisons, boards, predictions, exports and an API.

Rankings and data

Current and All-Time scores combine editorial judgement with tracked streaming data, as explained on Sources & method. They are an independent editorial index, not an official chart. Stream counts, chart positions and other figures come from third-party sources, are shown with their source dates and may be incomplete or change. Net worth figures are estimates from public reports. Information is provided for general interest and is not financial, legal or professional advice.

Accounts

Your content

You keep ownership of comments, corrections, receipts and boards you create. By posting public content you allow us to display it on the site. Do not post anything unlawful, abusive, misleading, infringing or containing other people's personal data. We may remove content and review reports.

Exports and API

Free exports and the API may be used with attribution to AFR/INDEX and the snapshot date. Pro API keys are personal, may be revoked, and are limited to 10,000 authenticated requests a day. Do not resell bulk data, try to bypass limits or overload the service. Third-party source data keeps its own attribution and usage terms.

Personal stats

Personal stats are built from data you choose to connect and are for your own use. Free access covers your top African artists and tracks, your taste profile and Music Receipt. Pro adds lifetime history import, the listening clock, full play history and Afri Wrapped. Spotify currently limits our app to a small number of approved testers, so Spotify login may not be available to you; Last.fm and history files are alternatives. Do not share another person’s listening data without their permission.
