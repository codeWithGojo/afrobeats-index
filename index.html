Discussion

The All-Time list ignores streams on purpose. Spotify did not exist when Fela, 2Baba and P-Square defined the sound. The nine-metric formula works across eras without platform data that only the modern generation has.

The Big 3 sit joint #1 because each owns a different part of the modern story: Burna the albums and stadiums, Davido the hits and the army, Wizkid the cool and the catalogue.

Current uses the exact same nine metrics as All-Time, then adds Streams as a 10th metric. That is the only difference. Newer acts with heavy platform numbers move up; legacy weight still counts.

Omah Lay is included with a full profile — Boy Alone and the emotional lane he owns are part of the modern story.
