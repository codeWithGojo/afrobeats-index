Discussion

Why All-Time excludes streams

All-Time compares careers across eras using nine editorial factors. Streaming totals are excluded because platform coverage is uneven across generations.

Why the Big 3 are tied

Burna Boy, Davido and Wizkid share first place by editorial decision. Their strengths differ across catalogue, cultural influence and live performance. This is a shared placement, not evidence of equal calculated scores.

How Current differs

Current combines the locked editorial contribution, normalized to 83 points, with up to 17 points from comparable tracked audio streams. It reflects the published snapshot; All-Time emphasizes career legacy.

Monthly listeners have no scoring weight. Current coverage is Spotify; unavailable providers are not estimated. YouTube views stay separate.

Release-year album & song lists

Eligibility: the 2026 page covers matched recordings and primary-artist projects dated 2026 in the checked catalogue. Albums, EPs and live projects need at least four tracks. Unreleased projects and unmatched credits are excluded. Coverage is limited to the Current 50 artist ledgers; this is not an exhaustive Africa-wide chart.

Albums: Kworb’s global tracked project totals, checked against Apple release identities and primary-artist credits. Songs: global recording totals in the artist ledgers. Both lists sort by total_streams; they do not measure calendar-year consumption. Album totals may include earlier advance-single plays. Nigeria Top 100 remains a separate weekly TurnTable chart. YouTube, Boomplay and Audiomack are not mixed into these Spotify totals.

Refresh: source checks are recorded separately from publisher updates. Each row links to its stream ledger, publisher date and release record. The header shows when Afri Index checked the sources. Historical editions retain their own coverage notes.

Awards & certifications audit

Awards: Wikipedia’s artist award table or dedicated “awards and nominations” list is used as the coverage guide. Where that guide publishes totals by award body, AFR/INDEX displays the complete body-level ledger plus total wins and nominations; individual highlighted results retain a direct source link. Wikipedia is a discovery guide, not the final authority.

Certifications: only Gold, Platinum, Multi-Platinum or Diamond entries matched to public recording-body registers are displayed. RIAA, BPI, Music Canada and SNEP register links remain visible on every profile, including profiles with no matched plaque in the current snapshot. Markets use different thresholds, so certifications are never combined into one global total.
